## 👨‍💻 About Me

I’m an **ERP Consultant, Systems Analyst, and Software Developer** specializing in **ERPNext and Frappe**. With **2+ years of hands-on experience**, I have contributed to the successful implementation and customization of multiple real-world systems for both for-profit and non-profit organizations across different sectors, working as part of experienced and collaborative teams.
Alongside enterprise solutions, I independently build **Flutter mobile applications** for finance and commerce, including **Lana wa Alayna** for accounting, debt management, reports, dashboards, local backups, and Google Drive cloud backup, and **Aqsati Pro** for multi-merchant installment sales, contracts, and payment tracking. 

I graduated **with Honors** from the Faculty of Computer and Information Technology at **Sana’a University**, where I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems with a GPA of **4.0 / 91.40%**.
